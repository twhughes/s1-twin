Ear test

Which one is closer to the reference?

You hear a reference sound, then A, then B. Choose the one that sounds more like the reference. When you cannot hear a difference, choose "Can't tell". There are no wrong answers: this checks the computer's idea of "close" against your ears.

40 pairs, about 10 minutes. Headphones help.
