    the disk-mode ritual
    1. Power the S-1 off.
    2. Hold [PLAY] while powering on — keep holding until the display shows the USB icon.
    3. Wait 1–2 min for a drive named S-1 to mount (this page will notice).
    4. Click WRITE TO S-1 (or copy the downloaded file into RESTORE/).
    5. Press [HOLD] on the S-1 and wait for dOnE.
    6. Power-cycle. Your pattern lives in the hardware.
