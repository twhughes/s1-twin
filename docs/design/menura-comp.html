Save to the S-1

The S-1 takes patterns over USB disk mode. Menura writes the file; the S-1 needs your hands for the rest.

  1. Power the S-1 off. Hold Play while you power it on.
  2. Wait one to two minutes for a drive named S-1 to appear.
  3. Pick a bank and slot below, then write the pattern.
  4. Press Hold on the S-1, wait for dOnE, then power-cycle it.

Load from the S-1

No drive named S-1 is mounted. Put the S-1 in disk mode to list its saved patterns here.
